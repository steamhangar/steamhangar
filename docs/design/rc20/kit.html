SteamHangar UI Kit

Proposal for web/css/kit.css and the scale in theme.css. Everything below is the only building material the page packages may use. The two page sections (Clients card, Downloads) sit in a 1400 px and a 400 px container; the same markup adapts via container queries.

Scale --fs-1..7 · --sp-1..8

--fs-1 11pxCaptions, mono meta, chips. Never below this.
--fs-2 12pxHints, pills, secondary lines
--fs-3 13pxBody text, labels, buttons
--fs-4 15pxCard titles, row names
--fs-5 18pxSection titles, card stats
--fs-6 24pxPage title
--fs-7 32pxHero number

Spacing on a 4 px grid: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 48. Radii stay --r-s 8, --r-m 12, --r-l 18. A node test rejects new raw font-size: Npx in app.css.

Contrast fix --dim-2

Old #63798A · 3.8:1 on surface

Caption text at 11 px. Fails WCAG AA for text.

New #7A91A0 · 5.0:1 on surface

Caption text at 11 px. Passes AA. Used only for tertiary text and grips.

Status vocabulary status-icon.js → .pill

Online Current Downloading Updating Verifying Update ready Agent outdated Not cached Partially cached Not verified Paused Cancelled Failed Idle Offline

The pill wraps the existing status-icon.js disc: shape first, word second, colour third (colour-blind safe, mockup round 5). Motion stays what it is today and means "happening right now": the download arrows fall, Updating and Verifying turn; everything else is still. prefers-reduced-motion stops it. New in the pill: a tinted background so the state reads at card size. Disc rule: the glyph box is 60 % of the disc diameter (16 px disc, 10 px glyph), so at least 3 px of disc stays visible around every shape.
